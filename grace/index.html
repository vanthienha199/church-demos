New around here?

Visiting a church for the first time can feel like walking into someone else's family dinner. We remember that feeling. So we keep it simple: good coffee, honest songs, a message from the Bible you can actually use on Monday, and people who will learn your name.

Tell us you are coming and I will make sure someone is at the front door to show you around.

  1. 01

    Parking

    Free lot behind the building, entrance on Lake Adair Blvd. First-time guests get the reserved spots by the front doors. Look for the clay-red signs.

  2. 02

    Kids check-in

    Come 10 minutes early to the Kids desk in the lobby. Every child gets a secure name tag, and every volunteer is trained and background checked.

  3. 03

    How long

    About 75 minutes. Songs, a short prayer, a message from the Bible, then coffee and pastries in the courtyard.

  4. 04

    What to wear

    Whatever you are comfortable in. You will see Sunday dresses and you will see flip-flops. It is Florida.

Ministries

Room for every age.

  • Kids

    Nursery to 5th grade during both Sunday services. Secure check‑in.

  • Students

    6th to 12th grade. Wednesdays at 7 PM, plus summer camp.

  • Women

    Tuesday morning Bible study and a brunch on the first Saturday.

  • Men

    Saturday 7 AM breakfast, then a few hours serving the neighborhood.

  • Life Groups

    Twelve groups in homes across Orlando, most weeknights.

Our beliefs

What we believe.

  1. One God

    Father, Son and Holy Spirit, who made the world and loves it.

  2. The Bible

    God's word to us: true, trustworthy, and the final word on how we live.

  3. Jesus

    He lived the life we could not, died for our sin, and rose again. We are saved by grace through faith in him.

  4. The church

    Not a building but a family, called to love our neighbors in Orlando and beyond.

  5. Hope

    Jesus will return and make all things new.
